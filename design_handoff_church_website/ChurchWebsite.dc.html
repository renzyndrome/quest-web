Who we are

One church that meets in many places.

Quest Laguna is a promise-driven church in the Laguna region of the Philippines. Each of our nine locations is led by its own pastor, and each community is built around small groups we call Quest Circles. So even as the church grows, it still feels like family.

Whether it's your first time in a church or you've walked with God for years, there is a place here to worship, belong, and grow.

Worship

We gather every Sunday across our nine locations to worship together and hear God's Word.

Community

Quest Circles are our small groups. A few people meet during the week to pray, study the Word, and look after one another.

Growth

Wherever you're starting from, someone here will walk with you as you take your next step of faith.
